Leaf Trait Syndrome Classification

Problem: Classify into discrete trait syndromes (sclerophyll, mesophyll, etc.)
Why neglected: Continuous traits are fashionable; discrete syndromes are "old school"
Features: SLA, LDMC, leaf N, leaf P, toughness
Actually interesting: Are functional types real or artifacts?
Goal from this research can be to find novel ways to handle models even with incomplete data.

Then we will be looking at the following Techiniques

1. Cascade-Correlation Networks (Fahlman & Lebiere, 1990)
Why forgotten: Completely superseded by backprop + SGD variants. Nobody uses this anymore.
What it was: Networks that grow during training - start with zero hidden units, add neurons one at a time when learning plateaus
Research angle: "Can cascade-correlation outperform fixed-architecture tiny NNs in resource-constrained environments?" Study if dynamic architecture growth beats pre-sized TINN for small models
Why interesting: TINN is fixed architecture - you could implement cascading on top of it
Data: Any classification benchmark (MNIST subset, UCI datasets)

2. Restricted Coulomb Energy (RCE) Networks

Why forgotten: Pre-deep learning pattern recognition method from late 80s. Dead end.
What it was: Prototype-based learning with "commitment" and "shrinking" - creates spherical decision regions
Research angle: Compare RCE vs TINN for few-shot learning scenarios
Feasible: Simple distance calculations, threshold logic
Modern twist: Hybrid RCE-TINN where RCE handles outliers, TINN handles main distribution

3. Counterpropagation Networks (Hecht-Nielsen, 1987)

Why forgotten: Combination of Kohonen layer + Grossberg layer. Nobody teaches this anymore.
What it was: Unsupervised + supervised hybrid, supposed to be faster than backprop
Research angle: "Rediscovering counterpropagation: When does vector quantization + interpolation beat gradient descent for tiny models?"
Very feasible: Self-organizing map feeding into linear output layer

4. Adaptive Resonance Theory (ART) Networks - ART1/ART2

Why forgotten: Complicated vigilance parameters, superseded by better clustering
What it was: Grossberg's solution to stability-plasticity dilemma - incremental learning without catastrophic forgetting
Research angle: "ART networks for continual learning on microcontrollers" - compare against TINN with naive fine-tuning
Modern relevance: Catastrophic forgetting is hot again, but nobody looks at ART anymore
Challenge: Somewhat complex to implement, but doable
